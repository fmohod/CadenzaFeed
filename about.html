About the Publication

About Cadenza Arthouse

Cadenza Arthouse is an independent media and editorial platform based in Houston, documenting the people, projects, and cultural work shaping art, music, wellness, and community life across the city.

What We Publish

We produce feature stories, interviews, and photography that document the people and spaces shaping local culture — work built through on-the-ground reporting, not press releases.

Why Cadenza Exists

Most of the work that shapes a city's culture goes undocumented. Artists, organizers, musicians, and small businesses build the things that define a community, and that work disappears without a record. Cadenza Arthouse exists to document it properly, and to keep it.

Archive, Not Just Publication

Every feature we publish becomes part of a permanent, growing archive — not a feed that disappears under the next post. We work with artists, organizers, performers, small businesses, and cultural projects to develop features that hold up as a lasting record.

Who This Is For

Cadenza Arthouse is for the people and organizations doing work worth documenting, and for the readers, collaborators, and partners trying to understand what's actually happening in Houston's creative community.

What Makes Us Different

We aren't advertising or promotion, and we aren't a personal blog. Cadenza Arthouse operates as a publication first: editorial selection, structured features, and an archive built to last — not content built to disappear in a feed.
