About the Publication

About Cadenza Arthouse

Cadenza Arthouse is an independent Houston publication dedicated to documenting the people, projects, and cultural work that often go unrecorded.

While much of a city's creative life disappears into social media feeds, temporary event listings, and fragmented online platforms, we believe the artists, organizers, musicians, educators, entrepreneurs, and community builders shaping a culture deserve a lasting record.

Through reporting, interviews, photography, and editorial features, Cadenza Arthouse documents the people and ideas helping define contemporary Houston and the communities connected to it.

We publish stories not because they are trending, but because they matter.

"We publish stories not because they are trending, but because they matter."

What We Publish

Cadenza Arthouse publishes:

  • Feature stories
  • Interviews
  • Photo essays
  • Event coverage
  • Community reporting
  • Cultural commentary
  • Artist and entrepreneur profiles

Our focus is on documentation, storytelling, and preservation rather than short-term engagement metrics.

Every story becomes part of a growing archive intended to remain valuable long after an event has ended.

Editorial Values

Documentation

We prioritize preserving cultural activity that might otherwise disappear without a record.

Independence

Coverage is selected based on editorial interest, community significance, and journalistic value — not popularity, status, or trends.

Curiosity

Meaningful stories are often found in unexpected places. We believe curiosity is essential to good reporting.

Community

We focus on the individuals, organizations, and projects actively shaping local culture and civic life.

Permanence

Our goal is to create an archive that remains useful years after publication.

Editorial Direction

Frankie MohammedFounder & Publisher
Cadenza Arthouse

Cadenza Arthouse was founded by Houston photographer and publisher Frankie Mohammed in 2017.

Drawing from a background in photography, journalism, community engagement, and cultural documentation, Frankie established Cadenza Arthouse as a platform dedicated to preserving stories that might otherwise be overlooked.

Today, the publication continues to combine reporting, interviews, photography, and long-form storytelling to create a growing archive of Houston's creative and community landscape.

Archive, Not Feed

Most online content is designed to disappear.

Cadenza Arthouse operates differently.

We believe stories, photographs, interviews, and cultural records should remain accessible long after they are published. Every feature contributes to a permanent archive documenting the people and projects shaping the communities we serve.

Our goal is not simply to report what happened.
Our goal is to preserve why it mattered.
