How We Work

Every published article is only one part of the archive. Behind each story are photographs, interviews, audio recordings, research, and verified documentation preserved for future generations.

01

Original Reporting

Stories are documented in person — on the ground, in the room, with the people who make Houston's culture.

02

Documentary Evidence

Photography, interviews, and recordings are gathered and verified before a story is published.

03

Permanent Preservation

Each article becomes a record in the Cadenza Arthouse Archive — institutional memory, not a feed that scrolls away.
