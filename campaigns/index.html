What a campaign is

A campaign is a story with a number on it. Cadenza Arthouse publishes reporting; a campaign is the piece where the publication itself says this specific thing should exist, here is why, and here is what it costs — and gives you a way to put money on it without leaving the page.

How the money moves

Every button on a campaign page is a Square checkout link owned by Cadenza Arthouse. Square processes the card; nothing about you is stored here. Progress figures on each page are maintained by Cadenza Arthouse from Square's records and dated, so you can see when they were last brought up to date. When a campaign buys something, the purchase is recorded on the campaign's own page, and what happens to it afterwards is appended there too.

What a campaign is not

Not a charity registration, and not a platform. Contributions are gifts to Cadenza Arthouse, a Houston company, in support of a stated objective. They are not tax-deductible unless a page says otherwise. Questions: operations@cadenzaarthouse.com.
