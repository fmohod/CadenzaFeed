Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is everyone guaranteed a feature?

No. Cadenza Arthouse selects stories based on relevance, timing, and available production capacity. We review every application carefully.

Is there a cost?

Some features are editorially selected at no cost. Expanded media services, commissioned content, and specialized coverage may involve fees — we'll discuss that after reviewing your application.

Do I need to be in Houston?

Houston-based stories receive priority consideration. Exceptional projects from elsewhere may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

Can businesses apply?

Yes. Businesses, nonprofits, artists, and community organizations are all welcome to apply.

Our Mission

Houston's Creative Community
Deserves Documentation

Too many artists, musicians, organizers, and cultural leaders do important work that is never properly documented. That work shapes neighborhoods, builds community, and defines what Houston actually is — and most of it disappears without a record.

Cadenza Arthouse exists to change that. Every feature becomes part of a lasting archive of the people shaping our communities today.
